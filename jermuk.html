From snow to bottle

What makes Jermuk taste the way it does.

  1. Snow and rain

    Water falls on the mountains around Jermuk.

  2. Through the rock

    It sinks deep into volcanic rock, warming and gathering minerals.

  3. Back to the surface

    It rises again at the Jermuk springs, naturally mineralised.

  4. Bottled at source

    It is bottled in Jermuk and reaches our warehouse in Yerevan.
