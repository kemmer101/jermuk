Good drinks, delivered properly.

Kemmer Solutions is a beverage distributor based in Yerevan. We keep a short, strong range and make sure it always arrives on time.

A small range we know inside out

We started with a simple idea: most venues in Armenia pour the same handful of drinks, so a supplier should be excellent at those rather than average at hundreds.

That is why Jermuk comes first. It is on nearly every table in the country, and running out of it is not an option. Around it we built a range of the Armenian beers people drink every day.

We hold stock in Yerevan, deliver with our own team and answer the phone when you call. That is the whole business.
